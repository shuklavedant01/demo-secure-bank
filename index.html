Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Secure Bank? We have compiled responses to common inquiries.

How secure is my money with Secure Bank?

Your money is protected under FDIC-equivalent deposit insurance guidelines up to ₹250,000 per depositor. Furthermore, we run continuous fraud monitoring, secure 256-bit encryption pipelines, and strict multi-factor authentication locks.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

No. We value absolute simplicity. There are zero charges for account opening, zero monthly subscription fees, and no minimum balance upkeep fees whatsoever.

How long does it take to open an account?

Our digital onboarding takes less than 2 minutes. Fill out your legal identity details, submit your PAN card number, and your account will be activated immediately.

Can I manage my credit card and FD online?

Yes. Our integrated internet banking portal lets you set up Fixed Deposits, apply for new loans, lock/unlock cards, set spending limits, and track rewards instantly.
