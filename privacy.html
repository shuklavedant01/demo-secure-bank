1. Data Fiduciary & Identity

Secure Bank (operating at demo-secure-bank.onrender.com) acts as the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). We process personal data solely for specified, lawful banking purposes based on explicit Data Principal consent or legal necessity.

2. Notice Requirements (§5 DPDP Act 2023)

Before or at the time of collecting personal data for account opening, loans, credit cards, or fixed deposits, we provide clear itemized notice specifying:

  • The specific personal data items requested (e.g. Full Name, PAN Number, Email, Mobile).
  • The specific purpose category (`P_ESSENTIALS`, `P_MARKETING`, `P_ANALYTICS`).
  • The manner in which Data Principals may exercise their rights or withdraw consent.

3. Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) Details

In accordance with §13 of the DPDP Act 2023, Data Principals may direct privacy complaints or unfulfilled withdrawal requests to our appointed officer:

Designation: Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO)

Official Email: gro@demo-secure-bank.onrender.com

Hotline: +91-1800-SECURE-BANK / +91-120-4570088

Response SLA: Within 48 Hours

4. Summary of Data Principal Rights

Under Sections 11–14 of the DPDP Act 2023, you retain full ownership over your digital footprint:

§11 Right to Access Access summary of data processed & third-party sharing details.
§12 Right to Correction & Erasure Correct inaccurate metrics or request complete data erasure.
§13 Right to Grievance Redressal Register complaints with GRO or Data Protection Board of India.
§14 Right to Nominate Nominate an individual to exercise rights in event of incapacity.
